# Full-Stack AI Developer — Page Plan

> **Route:** `/full-stack-ai-developer`
> **Status:** Planning
> **Linked from:** Upwork proposals for full-stack AI / SaaS / product engineering roles

---

## Page Objective

A dedicated proof page for Upwork prospects. They arrive with proposal context — this page provides **evidence, credibility, and confidence** to continue the conversation on Upwork.

**Success state:**

> Prospect quickly understands what I build → sees real production work → reads real client feedback → understands how I work → messages me on Upwork.

**Not:** a general portfolio, agency site, SEO page, or resume.

---

## Positioning

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Title** | Full-Stack AI Developer |
| **Core message** | I build and ship production-ready AI products, SaaS platforms, and custom software end-to-end. |
| **Voice** | First person — I / me / my. Founder-led, hands-on builder. |
| **Emphasis** | Product engineering → AI → SaaS → production work → technical ownership → client proof |
| **De-emphasize on this page** | n8n, GoHighLevel (they get their own dedicated pages) |

### Credibility bar (use sparingly — proof > claims)

- Top Rated Plus · $80K+ verified earnings · 100% Job Success
- 4,200+ hours · 37+ production contracts
- $24,500 AI platform · 5-star feedback · repeat & long-term clients

These stats support the page but should not dominate it. Let projects and reviews do the heavy lifting.

## Section 4 — Case Studies

**Purpose:** Deep proof on the strongest, most visually compelling projects. Demonstrates engineering capability + business understanding.

**Note:** Case studies ≠ production work grid. Select **2–4 projects** for depth. Others stay in the grid only.

### Recommended case study selection

| Priority | Project | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| **1** | UseCortana / AgentKong | AI agent platform, voice AI, highest AI relevance, strong contract value |
| **2** | MeisterFlow | Multi-tenant SaaS architecture — classic full-stack AI buyer concern |
| **3** | LogoArena | Long-term trust (1,754 hrs) — proves reliability at scale |
| **4** | Hiding Elephant | AI product, repeat client, visual product |

Real Estate Underwriting and Image Generation SaaS can remain grid-only unless strong screenshots become available.

### Case study template

Each case study follows:

```
Context        → What the product/business is
Problem        → What needed to be solved
What I built   → Concrete deliverables
My role        → What I personally owned
Complexity     → Integrations, architecture, edge cases (brief)
Outcome        → Result, client feedback snippet, or measurable impact
Visuals        → Screenshots, optional short video or workflow visual
```

Keep each case study scannable — no essay walls. Use subheadings and short paragraphs.
